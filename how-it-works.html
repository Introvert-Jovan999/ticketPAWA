How Ticket Pawa works

We prepare football betting slips and sell access to them. You pay us for the information. You place any bet yourself, separately, on betPawa.

From browsing to your code

  1. Choose what you want

    Buy a single ticket, or take a Daily, Weekly or Monthly plan. Pick one of three odds ranges: Lower, Balanced or High.

    Browse tickets

  2. Sign in with your phone number

    Create an account with your mobile number and a password. Accounts are for adults aged 25 and over, so we check your date of birth.

    Create an account

  3. Pay with Mobile Money

    Pay with MTN MoMo or Airtel Money. You approve the payment on your phone with your own PIN. We never see or ask for your PIN.

    See the checkout

  4. Receive your booking code

    Once the mobile money provider confirms your payment, the booking code unlocks in your account. A screenshot of a payment is never enough, because we check the real transaction.

    Go to my account

Every ticket shows the same information, so you can compare them quickly.

  • A
    Combined odds. All the selections multiplied together. Higher odds mean a lower chance of every pick landing.
  • B
    Selections. The number of matches on the slip. Open Details to see each match, market and its odds.
  • C
    Closes in. After this time the ticket is no longer sold. It is also the time to act before kickoffs begin.
  • D
    Booking code. Hidden until your payment is verified. It is then saved in your account to copy.

What you get, and what you don’t

What we do

 

  • Prepare and publish football slips
  • Show odds, matches and the time we last checked them
  • Release your booking code after verified payment
  • List every settled ticket as won, lost or void

What we never do

 

  • Promise or guarantee a win
  • Place bets for you or hold your betting money
  • Ask for your Mobile Money PIN
  • Belong to betPawa. Ticket Pawa is independent

Using your code on betPawa

  1. Copy your code from the ticket in your account.
  2. Open betPawa in its own app or website and look for the option to load a booking code.
  3. Check everything on the betPawa screen: the matches, the markets and the current odds.
  4. Decide for yourself. Odds may have changed since we published. Only bet an amount you can afford to lose.

Common questions

Does a ticket guarantee a win?

No. A ticket is information only. Any bet can lose, and higher odds carry more risk. Our Results page shows wins, losses and voids.

How soon do I get my code?

As soon as your Mobile Money payment is confirmed, usually within a minute. If it does not appear, contact us with your order reference.

What if the odds change on betPawa?

They can. We show the time we last checked the odds. Always look at the current odds on betPawa before you bet.

What if my payment fails?

If the payment is declined or times out, no money is taken and you can try again. If money left your account without a code, contact us with the reference.

Can I get a refund?

See the Refund policy for exactly when refunds apply.
