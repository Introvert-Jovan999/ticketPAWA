Plan rules in plain words

How many slips

Each day’s slips are listed on the plan card above. Sample: Lower and Balanced have 3 slips a day, High has 2.

When slips go live

New slips are normally published by 09:00 EAT (sample time). You will see the publish time on every ticket.

If no slip is published

If we publish no slip in your range on a day, that day is added to the end of your plan. This is the sample rule.

Questions

When does my plan start?

Your period begins when your payment is confirmed. The start and end time appear in your account, with a countdown.

Will a plan help me win?

No. A plan gives access to our published selections. Results are never guaranteed, and our Results page shows wins, losses and voids.

Does my plan renew by itself?

No. Plans are renewed by hand, so you are never charged without choosing to pay again.

Can I get a refund?

Refund rules are on the Refund policy page. Please read them before you pay.

Who can subscribe?

You must be 25 or older. Read our responsible gambling advice and never bet money you cannot afford to lose.

Prefer to buy one ticket?

Single tickets start from UGX 2,000 with no commitment.
